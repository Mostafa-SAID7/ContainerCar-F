# Drive Your Dream

ContainerCar Franchise Platform

Product Vision

Build a modern, production-quality web platform called ContainerCar.

ContainerCar is a business launch marketplace that helps people start a mobile business using a ready-to-build Container Car / Food Truck / Mobile Unit.

This is NOT a simple vehicle rental marketplace.

The core idea is:

"I have a budget. I want to start a business. Show me what business I can build, how much it costs, what I need, where I can operate, and help me start."

Example:

A user has:

300,000 EGP

Instead of researching dozens of suppliers, equipment vendors, franchise opportunities, and locations separately, ContainerCar gives them a structured business plan.

Example:

Coffee Truck

Investment:
280,000 EGP

Expected Setup:
45 days

Business Type:
Coffee

Estimated Monthly Operating Cost:
42,000 EGP

Staff:
2 people

Recommended Location:
High traffic / university / business district


The platform should transform:

Budget → Business Idea → Business Package → Setup → Launch
